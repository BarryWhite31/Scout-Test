How to read your results

  • Hot: under 5% equity after selling, or negative equity. Strong lease option lead.
  • Warm: 5% to 15% equity. Thin, and worth a call.
  • Cold: over 15% equity. The owner can usually sell normally.
  • Equity is an estimate: we assume the deposit you picked, a 25-year repayment mortgage at the typical rate for the year they bought, and 2% selling costs. The real mortgage is never public.
  • For warm and hot leads, buy the £7 title register on GOV.UK. It shows the owner, their contact address and their lender.
  • "2 agents" means the same home is listed by two different agents. The first agent's sole agency may have run out, or it could be a repossession.
  • Homes still on the market with an agent: go through the agent. Writing to owners directly works best for homes that have been withdrawn.

General information to help you decide which leads to look at. It isn't financial, legal or mortgage advice. Always check the facts yourself before acting on a lead.
